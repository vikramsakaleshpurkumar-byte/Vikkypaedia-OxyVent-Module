# Announcement — OxyVent: Oxygen and Ventilation (v4, 2026)

## Substack post

The most dangerous number on a children's ward is a normal saturation on oxygen. It tells you the blood is carrying oxygen. It does not tell you the child is still breathing well enough to clear carbon dioxide, and a drowsy child on a face mask can read 98% until shortly before an arrest.

I have rebuilt *OxyVent* around that idea. It has 20 units and runs from the pulse oximeter to the ventilator. It covers oxygen prescriptions that have a target and a failure trigger, cylinders that run out on the road, high-flow, bubble CPAP (including what the trials in Bangladesh, Malawi and Ethiopia really showed), the five diseases that fill our wards, and what to do in the first minute when a ventilated child suddenly worsens. Every unit says what a student, a ward nurse, a PICU nurse and a PG resident should take from it.

One rule is new. In the final assessment, a wrong answer to a safety-critical item (a displaced tube, a failing CPAP trial, an empty cylinder) means that attempt does not count, however high the score.

It is free and works offline on a phone.
